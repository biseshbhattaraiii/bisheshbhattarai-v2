# Building in Public: The Journey Begins

There's something powerful about building in public. It's vulnerable, exciting, and terrifying all at once.

## Why Now?

I've been building things for years, but mostly in private. Projects that never saw the light of day, ideas that stayed in notebooks, experiments that lived only on my local machine.

This changes today.

## The Plan

I'm committing to:

- Share my learnings as I discover them
- Document my projects, even the failed ones
- Write about the journey, not just the destination
- Be honest about what works and what doesn't

## What to Expect

This journal will be a mix of:

- Technical deep-dives when I learn something interesting
- Personal reflections on growth and discipline
- Travel stories and experiences
- Fitness progress and lessons

The format doesn't matter. The consistency does.

## Let's Go

If you're reading this, welcome. I hope you find something useful, or at least entertaining, in this journey.

Let's build something worth sharing.
