# Three Weeks in Nepal: Lessons from the Himalayas

The mountains don't care about your deadlines.

## Day One: Humbled Immediately

I thought I was fit. I run regularly, work out consistently. None of it mattered at 4,000 meters.

The altitude doesn't discriminate. It humbles everyone.

## What the Mountains Teach

**Patience**: You can't rush acclimatization. Your body adapts on its own schedule.

**Presence**: When every breath requires focus, you stop thinking about yesterday or tomorrow.

**Perspective**: Your biggest problems at sea level feel ridiculous at base camp.

## The People Along the Way

The Sherpa culture is remarkable. These people live and work at altitudes that would hospitalize most of us. They smile, they share tea, they carry impossible loads with grace.

I met a porter carrying 60kg of supplies. He was whistling.

## Coming Back Down

Descending is harder than ascending, both physically and mentally.

You've achieved the goal. Now what?

The real challenge is bringing the mountain's lessons back to normal life:

- Patience when things don't move at internet speed
- Presence when distractions multiply
- Perspective when minor inconveniences arise

## Would I Do It Again?

Without hesitation.

The mountains change you. Not in some mystical way, but practically. They show you what matters and what doesn't.

They remind you that you're small, but capable.

That's a lesson worth the climb.
